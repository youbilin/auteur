# Arc — release day

Create a 12-second, 16:9 product/UI motion graphics video for Arc, a release cockpit for small software teams.

Audience: engineers and product leads at 5-50 person startups.
Tone: calm, precise, editorial. Not hype. No purple gradients, no glassmorphism, no emoji.
Look: dusk (dark), accent #8BD3FF.
Key messages:
1. Release day without the noise.
2. See every change in a live preview before it ships.
3. Ship with confidence.

Use a laptop UI demo across three scenes:
- Scene 1: a releases workspace with deployments, rollbacks, and signals.
- Scene 2: a preview environment with a build-time chart and review checks.
- Scene 3: a final "ready to ship" summary with risk, coverage, and team.
Include at most one callout per scene. Keep copy short and concrete.
