# Orbit — mobile focus

Create a 12-second, 9:16 product/UI motion graphics video for Orbit, a mobile task app for focused work.

Audience: designers, developers, and makers.
Tone: warm, minimal, tactile. No emoji. No purple gradients.
Look: paper (light), accent #1F4FD8.
Key messages:
1. One task at a time.
2. A calm daily plan.
3. Finish the day clear.

Use a phone UI across three scenes: today's focus, a small progress chart, and an end-of-day summary.
Include one callout. Keep copy short.
