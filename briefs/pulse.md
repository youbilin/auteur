# Pulse — weekly signal

Create a 10-second, 16:9 product/UI motion graphics video for Pulse, a lightweight analytics dashboard for product teams.

Audience: product managers and founders.
Tone: analytical, quiet, confident. No hype. No purple gradients.
Look: paper (light), accent #1F4FD8.
Key messages:
1. Every metric in one calm view.
2. Spot the signal, not the noise.
3. Share a weekly brief in one click.

Use a dashboard UI with a headline, a bar chart, and a short list of insights.
Include one callout. Keep copy short.
