## Assets

- No third-party music or SFX are included. The ambient bed, whooshes, and ticks are synthesized with FFmpeg at render time.
- Chinese text uses a local system font via `@font-face { src: local(...) }`; no CJK font file is redistributed.
- No original Opus 5.5 videos are included. The ToDopa benchmark links to the public post and includes only Auteur's own output.
- Demo screenshots and MP4s in `docs/` and `bench/media/` were generated by Auteur.
