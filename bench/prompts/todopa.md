# ToDopa — proof of work

Public concept by @eta1ia: "BeReal for tasks." At the scheduled time, ToDopa rings until you show proof that you started the task. Proof earns a dopamine reward; willpower is replaced by a loop.

This is a concept adaptation, not a prompt reproduction. The original prompt is not public, and the original is a 59-second screen recording. This brief asks for a 12-second product film.

Create a 12-second, 9:16 product/UI motion graphics film.

- Audience: people who procrastinate and want a firmer start ritual.
- Tone: playful but disciplined. Not hype. No emoji.
- Look: dusk (dark), accent #4ADE80.
- Device: phone in every scene.
- Three scenes:
  1. A task is due. Show a timer, one task card, and a "proof required" state.
  2. Proof capture. Show a camera frame, an AI verification state, and a progress bar.
  3. Proof accepted. Show XP, a streak, and a clear reward state.
- Copy should be short and concrete. At most one callout per scene.
- Do not use Apple, OpenAI, or other trademarked product names. Use "AI verification" or "proof check" instead.
- Brand: ToDopa, url todopa.app, tagline "Start before you feel ready."
